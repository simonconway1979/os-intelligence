V1 is the open-source version of OS-Intelligence: a reasoning layer for one product manager. It remembers your product work between sessions and turns your meetings, documents and notes into one current picture of each project.

  • Free and open source, under Apache 2.0.
  • Runs in Claude Code. Setup takes about 15 minutes, and no coding skills are needed.
  • Your files stay on your own machine.
  • The GitHub page explains it step by step, including a 60-second look at a made-up example project.
